What BidShield is — and isn't.

It stress-tests your assumptions.

BidShield does not invent a universal cleaning price. It uses the price, labor, costs and minimum margin you enter, then shows how much error the bid can survive.

It is a pre-sign decision tool.

The core question is not “what should cleaning cost?” It is “if my estimate is wrong, when does this contract stop meeting my margin floor?”
